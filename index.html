            Detect purchases automatically?

            • What it does: when your bank texts you about a card purchase, you get a notification like “New purchase: 45.00 SAR at Starbucks”. Tap it to add the expense, already filled in.
            • What it reads: only messages from the bank senders you choose. Everything else is ignored, and one-time passwords and verification codes are never processed.
            • Privacy: messages are read on this phone only and never sent anywhere. The app keeps just the amount, merchant, card’s last 4 digits and time, never the message itself.
            • Permissions: Android will ask to let the app receive SMS, and to show notifications. If you say no, the app still works as before, and you can turn this on later.
